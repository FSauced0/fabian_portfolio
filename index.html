About

Engineering with a systems perspective.

I earned my B.S. in Aerospace Engineering from The University of Texas at Austin. My work has included aerodynamics, computational fluid dynamics, aircraft design, structural analysis, wind-tunnel testing, flight testing, and technical documentation.

I am especially interested in using computational and experimental methods together: building models, testing assumptions, interpreting data, and turning results into better engineering decisions.

Research & Experience

Selected engineering experience

Senior Design • UT Austin

Aerodynamics & Structural Lead — VTOL Wildfire Aircraft

Led aerodynamic and structural design work for a VTOL aircraft developed for a wildfire-response mission. Supported wing and empennage design, aircraft performance, weight and CG tracking, risk assessment, manufacturing, structural testing, and flight testing.

Undergraduate Research

Airfoil Aerodynamics & Wind-Tunnel Testing

Investigated vortex-generator effects on a Clark Y-14 airfoil using pressure measurements, force data, lift/drag analysis, and iterative improvements to the experimental model.

Coursework & Technical Projects

High-Speed Aerodynamics, CFD & Flight Mechanics

Applied numerical and experimental methods to compressible flow, shock-wave behavior, aircraft performance, stability, controls, and aerodynamic analysis.

About

Engineering with a systems perspective.

I earned my B.S. in Aerospace Engineering from The University of Texas at Austin. My work has included aerodynamics, computational fluid dynamics, aircraft design, structural analysis, wind-tunnel testing, flight testing, and technical documentation.

I am especially interested in using computational and experimental methods together: building models, testing assumptions, interpreting data, and turning results into better engineering decisions.
